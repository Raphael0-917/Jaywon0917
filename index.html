Research direction

From recovery to reliable decisions.

My interest is not only in detecting failure, but in deciding how much intervention is actually necessary and how much trust should be placed in that decision.

01

Current FYP

Offline trajectory data with artificially introduced XYZ errors are used to investigate efficient learning-based corrections. The work is still in progress, and the data pipeline, model design, and evaluation are being refined.

02

Long-term question

Can a robot estimate which recovery options are likely to succeed, understand how trustworthy those predictions are, and choose the least costly intervention that still satisfies a required level of task reliability?

03

Why it matters

A failure warning alone does not tell a robot what to do next. A small realignment may be enough in one state, while another state may require a stronger recovery policy. The decision should reflect both reliability and cost.

04

How I want to grow

I want to deepen my theoretical understanding of uncertainty and probabilistic decision-making, while learning how to validate these ideas rigorously on real robotic systems.
